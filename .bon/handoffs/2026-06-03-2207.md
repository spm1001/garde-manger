## For Claudes to come

A count that gates a destructive action can be wrong from **granularity**, not only staleness or key-namespace: garde's "2,267 sessions" were 2,267 per-file keep-*decisions* — ~761 were the same long sessions resumed across project dirs, so the true unique corpus was 1,763. The count was current and correct *as rows*, wrong *as the noun it was read as* (sessions). Before banking a count that gates an `rm`, ask not just "right namespace?" but "right grain — rows, or unique entities?"
